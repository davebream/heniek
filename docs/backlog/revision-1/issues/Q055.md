# Q055 — Implement retention, archive, export/import and backup/restore

## Outcome

Retention and archive policy safely prune eligible data, while portable run/workspace exports and full backup/restore preserve integrity without secrets.

## Specification anchors

- §26 Retention, backup, and export
- §27.4 Secrets and logs
- §18 Durability and recovery

## Dependency

Requires Q054 to be merged and independently confirmed on remote `main`.

## Tier

`T2-capability`

## Constraints

- Exports are versioned, checksummed, and previewable.
- Destructive retention requires eligibility proof and audit events.
- Preserve Product Specification v0.2 commitments and acceptance criteria.
- Keep Windmill, predecessor automation tooling, and TAKT out of Heniek runtime dependencies.
- Use strict ESM TypeScript, provider-neutral public contracts, and repository conventions.

## Exclusions

- No credential export.
- No promise to resume live provider processes from a portable bundle.
- No factory runtime state, credentials, transcripts, or control artifacts in this repository.
- No unrelated backlog work or speculative scope reduction.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Archive/prune decisions respect active references, legal holds, and configured retention.
- [ ] Export/import round-trips contracts, artifacts, events, snapshots, and repository/base metadata.
- [ ] Backup/restore handles schema migration, corruption detection, and atomic replacement.
- [ ] Public contracts and generated schemas remain compatible or are versioned deliberately.
- [ ] `pnpm check` passes from a clean checkout on Node.js 24.
- [ ] The pull request is non-draft, self-contained, and uses `Closes <this issue>`.

## Required tests

- Retention eligibility and reference-reachability property tests.
- Cross-version export/import and backup corruption/fault-injection tests.
- Regression tests for every defect discovered while implementing this issue.

## Required evidence

- Round-trip checksum manifest.
- Backup restore and retention audit report.
- Exact commands and results for local checks.
- GitHub required-check and merge confirmation.

## Autonomous stop conditions

Stop with a typed blocker and evidence if the predecessor is not merged, queue
markers disagree, a product commitment would need to be narrowed, a required
subscription route cannot be proven, credentials could be exposed, or a genuine
technical ambiguity remains after the issue's bounded spike/ADR path.
